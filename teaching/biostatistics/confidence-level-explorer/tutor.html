One normal distribution and its confidence limits

The curve is the sampling distribution of x̄, centered at the entered sample mean. The shaded central area is the chosen confidence level; the two boundaries correspond to −z* and +z* and map to the absolute confidence limits.

Confidence intervals from repeated large samples

Each horizontal segment is a confidence interval from a simulated sample. Bright blue intervals include μ; red intervals miss μ. Hover a segment for its exact values.

How to read this graph. The vertical dark line is the fixed population mean μ. A confidence interval contains μ when its horizontal segment crosses that line. Over many replications, approximately the selected confidence percentage should contain μ.
